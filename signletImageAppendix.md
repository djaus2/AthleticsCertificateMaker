# Appendix A - Reconstructed Request History

The following list provides a historical record of the image enhancement requests and outcomes that led to the final certificate background image.

## Initial Request


**Request**

Use a photograph of the athletics singlet as a visual element within participation certificates.

**Outcome**

A watermark-style background image was selected as the preferred approach.

---

## Background Removal

**Request**

Remove the photographic background and isolate the singlet.

**Outcome**

A clean cut-out version of the singlet was produced that could be positioned on the certificate without distractions.

---

## Wrinkle Reduction

**Request**

Reduce visible wrinkles, folds and shadows in the singlet fabric.

**Outcome**

The garment appearance was cleaned up to create a more professional presentation and improve readability of overlaid certificate text.

---

## Watermark Appearance

**Request**

Make the singlet less dominant so that certificate wording remains the primary focus.

**Outcome**

Opacity was progressively reduced until a suitable watermark effect was achieved.

The approved version used approximately 60% opacity.

---

## Positioning Adjustments

**Request**

Centre the singlet within the certificate layout.

**Outcome**

The singlet was positioned centrally behind the certificate text and name fields.

---

## Sizing Adjustments

**Request**

Increase the size of the singlet so that it becomes a significant design element.

**Outcome**

The singlet was enlarged and scaled proportionally to provide a balanced appearance across the page.

---

## Logo Review

**Request**

Review the logos visible on the singlet and determine whether they should remain.

**Outcome**

A decision was made that embedded logos taken from a photograph were not ideal for ongoing use.

Reasons included:

- Potential copyright concerns.
- Potential trademark concerns.
- Poor reproduction quality.
- Difficulty maintaining branding standards.

---

## Logo Removal

**Request**

Remove the logos from the singlet image.

**Outcome**

The singlet image was cleaned up by removing logo artwork to create a neutral background image.

---

## Official Logo Acquisition

**Request**

Obtain original logo artwork rather than extracting logos from the photograph.

**Outcome**

The preferred solution became:

- Obtain official logo files from the organisation.
- Replace photographic logos with supplied artwork.
- Use transparent PNG or SVG versions wherever available.

---

## Victorian Masters Athletics Logo

**Request**

Use a separate high-quality Victorian Masters Athletics logo rather than relying on the logo visible in the singlet photograph.

**Outcome**

The logo was incorporated as an independent certificate element.

Benefits included:

- Higher image quality.
- Better scaling.
- Easier positioning.
- Future branding flexibility.

---

## Certificate Readability Review

**Request**

Confirm that all certificate text remains easy to read when displayed over the singlet background.

**Outcome**

Certificate text remained clearly readable while retaining the visual impact of the singlet image.

---

## Final Approved Design

The final approved design consisted of:

- White certificate background.
- Large centred singlet watermark.
- Approximately 60% singlet opacity.
- Removed photographic logos.
- Separate Victorian Masters Athletics logo.
- Centrally aligned certificate text.
- Automated rendering from PowerShell certificate generation scripts.

---

# Key Recommendations for Future Updates

When preparing replacement singlet images:

1. Start with the highest resolution photograph available.
2. Remove backgrounds before making other edits.
3. Reduce visible wrinkles and shadows.
4. Remove logos obtained from photographs.
5. Request official logo artwork from the logo owner.
6. Prefer SVG format where available.
7. Maintain readability over artistic appearance.
8. Test against long participant names.
9. Test on both desktop and mobile devices.
10. Keep a copy of the original source photograph for future reprocessing.
